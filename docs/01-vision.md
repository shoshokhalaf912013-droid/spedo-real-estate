# SPEDO Vision

Version: 2.0

Last Updated: June 2026

---

# Introduction

SPEDO is not a traditional real estate website.

It is a complete Real Estate Investment Platform designed around a modern engineering architecture that transforms official developer documents into reliable, searchable, analyzable, and investment-ready digital assets.

The website is only one consumer of the platform.

The true product is the SPEDO Core.

---

# Vision

To become the most trusted real estate investment platform in Egypt by providing verified, structured, transparent, and continuously updated property data directly from official developer sources.

Accuracy will always be more valuable than quantity.

Trust will always be more valuable than marketing.

Engineering quality will always be more valuable than rapid development.

---

# Mission

Transform raw developer information into intelligent digital knowledge.

SPEDO simplifies the entire investment journey by allowing investors to discover, compare, analyze, and purchase real estate using verified information instead of advertisements.

---

# Platform Philosophy

SPEDO is a Data-Driven Platform.

The system is not built around pages.

It is built around structured data.

Pages are generated from data.

Search works on data.

Comparison works on data.

Analytics work on data.

Investment tools work on data.

Artificial Intelligence works on data.

The data itself is always the single source of truth.

---

# Core Architecture Philosophy

The platform is divided into independent layers.

```
Application
        │
Runtime
        │
Kernel
        │
Services
        │
Engines
        │
Repositories
        │
Projects
        │
Units
```

Every layer has one responsibility.

No layer is allowed to bypass another.

This separation guarantees maintainability, scalability, and long-term stability.

---

# Single Source of Truth

Every value inside SPEDO must have exactly one official source.

No duplicated values.

No manually invented prices.

No estimated specifications.

No hidden business logic inside pages.

Developer documents always win.

Whenever the official source changes, SPEDO updates automatically.

---

# Official Data Sources

Projects are generated only from verified developer material.

Examples include:

- Official Excel Files
- Official Price Lists
- Official Brochures
- Official Floor Plans
- Official Master Plans
- Official Payment Plans
- Official Developer Documents

Nothing else.

---

# Engineering Principles

Every decision inside SPEDO follows these principles.

## 1. Data First

Data always comes before UI.

---

## 2. Core Before Website

Business logic belongs inside the Core.

The website only consumes services.

---

## 3. Modular Design

Every feature lives inside its own module.

Modules must be replaceable.

Modules must be testable.

---

## 4. Strong Typing

Every object has a defined type.

No unknown structures.

No hidden fields.

---

## 5. Scalability

Adding one hundred projects must require no architectural changes.

Only data should grow.

Never complexity.

---

## 6. Testability

Every Engine.

Every Service.

Every Repository.

Must be independently testable.

---

## 7. Reusability

Business logic should never be duplicated.

Everything should be reusable.

---
